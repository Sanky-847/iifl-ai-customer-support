# IIFL Finance - Loan Foreclosure & Prepayment Policy

## 1. Foreclosure & Prepayment Overview
Borrowers may choose to pay off their loan before the end of the contracted loan tenure through **Part-Prepayment** (paying a portion of loan principal ahead of time) or **Foreclosure / Full Pre-closure** (paying off the entire remaining loan principal balance).

---

## 2. Personal Loan Prepayment & Foreclosure Terms
- **Lock-in Period:** Minimum 6 mandatory EMI payments must be completed before foreclosure or part-prepayment is allowed.
- **Foreclosure Charges:**
  - Foreclosure within 7–12 months: 4.0% of the principal outstanding + GST.
  - Foreclosure after 12 months: 2.0% of the principal outstanding + GST.
  - Foreclosure using borrower's own funds (non-refinanced): Charges waived as per regulatory guidelines for floating rate loans.
- **Part-Prepayment Limits:** Minimum ₹10,000 per transaction; maximum twice per financial year.

---

## 3. Gold Loan Foreclosure Terms
- **Lock-in Period:** No lock-in period. Borrowers can foreclose a Gold Loan at any time without penalty.
- **Foreclosure Charges:** 0% (Nil foreclosure fee or prepayment penalty for Gold Loans).
- **Interest Recalculation:** Interest is charged strictly for the exact number of days the loan remained active.

---

## 4. Procedure for Foreclosure
To initiate a loan foreclosure:
1. Submit a formal Foreclosure Request at the home branch or via the IIFL Customer Portal.
2. Request an official **Foreclosure Statement** showing total principal, interest accrued up to the foreclosure date, and applicable charges.
3. Make full payment via NEFT/RTGS/UPI or Demand Draft.
4. Obtain the No Objection Certificate (NOC) and pledge collateral return (if applicable) within 7 working days.

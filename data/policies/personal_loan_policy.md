# IIFL Finance - Personal Loan Policy

## 1. Overview & Loan Eligibility
IIFL Finance offers unsecured Personal Loans to salaried and self-employed individuals to meet personal financial requirements.

### Eligibility Criteria:
- **Age:** Minimum 21 years at loan application; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Employment Type:** Salaried employee at an eligible private/public company or Self-Employed Professional/Business Owner.
- **Minimum Monthly Income:** ₹25,000 net per month for salaried; ₹3,500,000 annual turnover for self-employed.
- **Minimum Credit Score (CIBIL):** 700 or above.
- **Work Experience:** Minimum 1 year total work experience with at least 6 months at current employer.

---

## 2. Loan Amount and Tenure
- **Minimum Loan Amount:** ₹50,000
- **Maximum Loan Amount:** ₹25,000,000 (Subject to income multiplier and credit evaluation)
- **Repayment Tenure:** Minimum 12 months (1 year) to Maximum 60 months (5 years).

---

## 3. Interest Rates & Processing Fees
- **Interest Rate Range:** 11.75% to 22.00% per annum (floating or fixed based on credit profile).
- **Processing Fee:** Up to 2.50% of the sanctioned loan amount + applicable GST.
- **Documentation Charges:** Flat ₹500 + GST.
- **Late Payment Penalty:** 2.0% per month on the overdue EMI amount.

---

## 4. Documentation Required
Applicants must submit the following documents during application:
1. **Identity Proof:** PAN Card (Mandatory), Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
2. **Address Proof:** Aadhaar Card, Recent Utility Bill (Electricity/Water within 3 months), or Rent Agreement.
3. **Income Proof:**
   - Salaried: Salary slips for the last 3 months and Bank statements for the last 6 months showing salary credit.
   - Self-Employed: Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 2 financial years with computation of income, and Bank statements for the last 12 months.
4. **Employment / Business Proof:** Official Email ID, Employee ID Card, or Business Registration Certificate.

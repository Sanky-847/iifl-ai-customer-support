# IIFL Finance - Gold Loan FAQ & Policy

## 1. What is an IIFL Gold Loan?
An IIFL Gold Loan is a secured loan provided against gold jewelry pledged as collateral. It offers instant approval with minimal documentation.

---

## 2. Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio & Valuation
- **Maximum LTV Ratio:** Up to 75% of the current market value of gold, adhering to Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines.
- **Accepted Gold Purity:** Gold jewelry/ornaments of 18 to 22 Karats purity. Gold coins above 50g or bullion bars are not accepted.
- **Valuation Process:** In-branch certified gold evaluation conducted in front of the customer using non-destructive testing.

---

## 3. Interest Rates & Repayment Options
- **Starting Interest Rate:** 9.50% per annum onwards.
- **Repayment Schemes:**
  1. **Bullet Repayment:** Pay principal and total interest together at loan maturity.
  2. **Monthly Interest Payment:** Pay monthly interest servicing with principal repaid at loan maturity.
  3. **Standard EMI:** Equal monthly installments covering principal and interest.
- **Tenure:** Flexible options ranging from 3 months to 24 months.

---

## 4. Gold Security & Release Process
- **Security Standard:** Pledged gold is stored in high-security vaults protected by 24/7 CCTV surveillance, electronic access control, and full insurance cover at no extra charge to the borrower.
- **Gold Release:** Pledged gold is returned to the account holder immediately upon full repayment of outstanding principal, interest, and charges.
- **ID Verification for Pickup:** Original borrower must present Government ID (PAN/Aadhaar) and original physical receipt during redemption.
